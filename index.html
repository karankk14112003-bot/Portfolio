About Me

Put a number on it.
Then test the number.

I studied the MSc Finance at UCD Michael Smurfit Graduate Business School (2:1) after a first-class B.Com in Accounting & Finance from SRM Institute of Science & Humanities in Chennai. Most of my work sits where valuation, risk and econometrics meet. I build the model first and then check how well it holds up against the data.

On the MSc I valued Air Products & Chemicals with a full FCFF/DCF and trading-comps model on Bloomberg data, wrote a 1,845-line Python framework that backtests eight VaR and Expected Shortfall models on the DJIA, and replicated an information-asymmetry study across 779 US M&A deals in Python and Stata.

I now work as Business Development Manager at Relocate to Ireland, where I built the company's first pricing model. Before the MSc I spent a year in finance operations at ABART Enterprises in Chennai, working on monthly P&L, reconciliations and the AR/AP cycle. I am SAP FICO and Tally certified, based in Dublin, and open to Financial Analyst, Equity Research and Investment Banking roles in Ireland and across the EU. I speak English, Tamil, Hindi and Malayalam.
